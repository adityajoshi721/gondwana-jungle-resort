Privacy and analytics

Optional Google Analytics helps us understand which pages and experiences are useful. It is loaded only after you allow analytics. When configured, use “Privacy choices” on our main pages to change your choice. Declining analytics does not prevent enquiries.

What is measured

Page visits, approximate device and traffic-source information, sections viewed, gallery and planner interactions, video playback milestones, and contact-button activity. Google Analytics uses browser identifiers to estimate new and returning visitors. These are estimates, not a count of identified people.

Our analytics events exclude enquiry names, email addresses, phone numbers, dates, messages and WhatsApp message URLs. We send a page URL without its query string and a referrer domain rather than its full URL. Campaign names may be collected; please do not put personal information in campaign tags.

Enquiries and external services

The details you choose to submit are used to respond to your enquiry. WhatsApp requests are sent only when you send the prepared message in WhatsApp. Where configured, EmailJS handles email enquiries. Google Maps, Google Fonts and linked services have their own privacy practices; the analytics choice does not control those services.

Your choice

Your analytics preference is stored in this browser. Withdrawing stops future analytics collection and attempts to clear our Google Analytics cookies; it does not automatically erase previously collected data held by Google. For a privacy enquiry, contact gondwana357@gmail.com.
